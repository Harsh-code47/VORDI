# 🎙️ VORDI — Voice Browser Extension (v1.0.0)

> Control your entire web browser hands-free with voice commands and the wake word **"Hey VORDI"**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Hands-Free Wake Word:** Keep the **VORDI Voice Hub** open in the Side Panel or pinned tab, and simply say **"Hey VORDI"** anytime while browsing!
- **Tap to Speak:** Instant voice command execution from the toolbar popup or the on-screen floating HUD.
- **Global Hotkey (`Alt+V`):** Press **Alt + V** from anywhere in Chrome to trigger voice commands instantly.
- **Tab Management:** Open, close, switch, reload, mute, pin, and duplicate tabs hands-free.
- **Natural Web Navigation:** Go directly to websites or perform searches (*"search for quantum computing"*).
- **DOM & Page Control:** Click buttons, follow links, type into inputs, submit forms, and scroll.
- **Media Playback:** Play/pause videos, seek forward/back, adjust volume, and toggle fullscreen/PiP.
- **Voice Macros:** Record and replay automated multi-step browser tasks.
- **Live Visual Audio Meter:** Visual confirmation that your microphone is actively hearing your voice.

---

## 🛠️ Installation & Setup (Under 1 Minute)

1. **Open Extensions Page:**
   - In Google Chrome, navigate to:
     ```
     chrome://extensions/
     ```
2. **Enable Developer Mode & Reload:**
   - Turn on the **Developer mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
   - Click the **🔄 Reload** icon on the VORDI card (or click **Load unpacked** and select `D:\VORDI\extension`).
3. **Grant Microphone Permission:**
   - Click the VORDI extension icon in your toolbar, click **Enable Mic**, and click **Allow** on Chrome's prompt.

---

## 🗣️ How to Use Hands-Free "Hey VORDI" (Voice Hub)

In Chrome, background web pages are restricted from accessing your microphone silently. To enable true hands-free voice control across all your tabs:

1. Click the VORDI toolbar icon and click **"🎙️ Open Hands-Free Voice Hub"** (or open the **Side Panel**).
2. The Voice Hub will open. Notice the **green audio meter moving** as you speak.
3. Now switch to **any other tab** and browse normally.
4. Speak out loud anytime:
   > *"Hey VORDI, new tab"*
   > *"Hey VORDI, search for artificial intelligence"*
   > *"Hey VORDI, scroll down"*
   > *"Hey VORDI, go to wikipedia.org"*
   > *"Hey VORDI, close tab"*

VORDI will hear you from the Voice Hub, speak voice confirmation, and execute the command on your active tab!

## 🔬 Self-Test & Troubleshooting

If your voice is not being heard:
1. Open the Voice Hub and click **"🔬 Run Audio & Microphone Self-Test"**.
2. Check if your microphone volume is turned up in Windows Settings -> System -> Sound -> Input.
3. Ensure you are using Google Chrome (Brave browser disables Google Speech by default).
