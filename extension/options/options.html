Privacy & Data Security

VORDI is engineered with privacy as a first-class citizen.

🛡

Zero Audio Storage

Audio is processed in real-time through the browser's native Web Speech API. VORDI never uploads raw audio recordings to any server.

🏠

100% Local Configuration

All settings, voice macros, and recent command logs are strictly stored inside your browser's local sandbox (chrome.storage.local).
